How It Works

Create Visuals in 4 Simple Steps

No signup, no downloads, no complicated setup. Just pure visualization magic.

1

Upload Your Music

Drag and drop any audio file (MP3, WAV, OGG, FLAC, M4A) or click to browse. Your files stay private and are never uploaded to any server.

2

Choose Your Mode

Cycle through 8 unique visualization modes to find the perfect style for your music. Each mode responds differently to various frequencies.

3

Customize the Look

Fine-tune colors, sensitivity, beat detection, and visual effects. Save your favorite presets to use again later.

4

Export & Share

Record your visualization as a video file with your music included. Export in up to 4K quality and share on YouTube, TikTok, or Instagram.
