Features

Everything you need to visualize music

Professional-grade audio visualization tools — completely free, running directly in your browser.

🎬

4K video export

Export your visualizations as high-quality videos with your music included. Perfect for social media and content creation.

💓

Beat detection

Advanced algorithms detect beats in real time, creating pulses that sync perfectly with your music's rhythm.

Real-time FFT

How it works

Create visuals in 4 simple steps

No signup, no downloads, no complicated setup — just open the app and start creating.

1

Upload your music

Drag and drop any audio file (MP3, WAV, OGG, FLAC, M4A). Files stay private and never leave your device.

2

Choose a mode

Cycle through 8 unique visualization modes to find the perfect style for your music.

3

Customize the look

Fine-tune colors, sensitivity, and beat detection. Save your favourite presets for next time.

4

Export & share

Record your visualization as a video with audio in up to 4K and share it on YouTube, TikTok, or Instagram.
