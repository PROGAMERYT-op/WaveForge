WaveForge - Free Online Music Visualizer

WaveForge is a professional-grade, browser-based music visualizer and audio spectrum analyzer. Transform your favorite songs into mesmerizing visual experiences with our powerful Web Audio API technology.

Key Features

  • 8 Stunning Visualization Modes: Bar Spectrum, Waveform, Radial EQ, Particles, Spectrogram, 3D Bars, DNA Helix, and Galaxy
  • Real-time FFT audio analysis with customizable resolution
  • Advanced beat detection for dynamic visual reactivity
  • Video export with audio - save your visualizations as MP4/WebM
  • Microphone input for live audio visualization
  • 6 beautiful color palettes including Cyberpunk, Inferno, and Rainbow
  • High contrast mode for accessibility
  • Keyboard shortcuts for power users
  • Works offline - no internet required after loading
  • 100% free with no registration required

Supported Audio Formats

WaveForge supports all major audio formats including MP3, WAV, OGG, FLAC, AAC, and M4A. Simply drag and drop your audio file or click the upload button to get started.

How to Use WaveForge

  1. Upload your audio file using the "Open File" button or drag and drop
  2. Press play to start the visualization
  3. Use the "Mode" button to switch between 8 visualization styles
  4. Customize colors and effects in the Config panel
  5. Export your creation as a video with the Export button

Keyboard Shortcuts

  • Space - Play/Pause
  • S - Stop
  • M - Change Mode
  • F - Fullscreen
  • Arrow Left/Right - Seek 5 seconds
  • Arrow Up/Down - Volume control
